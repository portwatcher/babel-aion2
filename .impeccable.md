# Babel interface context

## Design Context

### Users

AION2 Steam Global players in Asia use Babel during battles. They enter a game
nickname, listen to a Discord commander, and read subtitles in their chosen
language. Administrators configure the room through a separate Access-protected
page. The original product brief is in `design.md`.

### Brand Personality

Clear, calm, dependable. The user selected a dark, low-distraction battle panel.
No official Babel logo was supplied.

### Aesthetic Direction

Reuse the PrimeVue components and Aura family used by the related Perohub admin.
Use a dark theme with restrained emphasis, clear form labels, and honest empty
states. Preserve normal browser text scaling and keyboard operation. Inherit
the established component typography; the final Babel brand palette is unset.

### Design Principles

1. Keep entry, audio status, and subtitle configuration easy to identify.
2. Use explicit labels and short recovery instructions for unavailable services.
3. Show real state only. Do not add sample players, subtitles, or enabled languages.
4. Share controls and tokens across future Babel applications.
5. Keep administration outside the player flow.
